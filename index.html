01 — The gap

Good businesses deserve
a better digital presence.

You have spent years on the details. The menu, the room, the light at seven in the evening, the way people feel when they walk in. Most hospitality businesses get that part right.

Then someone finds you on a phone, and none of it comes through. A slow page. A scattered feed. Photographs from two concepts ago. A booking link that leads nowhere.

The business is not the problem. The way it is presented is. North Frame exists to close that distance — so the digital side of the business feels as considered as the business itself.

The Ticket

Most hospitality businesses end up with a web designer, a content creator and somebody running ads. Three people, three directions, no shared plan — and a digital presence that quietly works against itself.

The Ticket to Scale brings the parts that matter into one place: the website, the positioning, the social presence and the paid advertising, built and run as a single system.

Four steps,
one direction.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business, positioning and goals. What it is, who it is for, and what it is actually trying to grow.

  2. 02

07 — About

Built with intention.

Youssef Cherif — Founder, North Frame

North Frame started from a simple observation: the hospitality businesses I admired most were often the ones represented worst online.

I built it to do that part properly — to present a business the way it deserves to be seen, and to give it one clear digital direction instead of four disconnected services pulling in different ways.

That is still the whole idea. Fewer moving parts, better decisions, work that holds up when you look closely.
